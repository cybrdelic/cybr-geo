# Motor source record — checked 2026-09-09

Selected reference: **ODrive M8325s 100KV**, a radial-flux outrunner in a compact pancake form. The reconstruction uses published external dimensions and visually inspected manufacturer photographs. This is a source-based reconstruction, not a downloaded factory assembly.

## Primary sources

1. Manufacturer product page and photograph gallery: https://shop.odriverobotics.com/products/m8325s
2. Manufacturer motor characteristics: https://docs.odriverobotics.com/v/latest/hardware/odrive-motors.html
3. Manufacturer S1/M8325S kit guide: https://docs.odriverobotics.com/v/latest/guides/s1-kit.html
4. Public CAD link exposed by the product page: https://cad.onshape.com/publications/e82046de83f355995ed44197/w/9343155368e2d977d01ea4e0/e/30b60213f56701824ffe6972

The Onshape publication was located, but its native CAD was **not exported or incorporated**. The files in `outputs/m8325s/` were generated by this project's own procedural source. The manufacturer photographs are linked for provenance rather than bundled as if they were our rendered work.

## Dimensions and physical data used

| Item | Published value | Treatment in model |
|---|---|---|
| Outside diameter | 92 mm | Exterior dimensional anchor |
| Mounting plane to front plane | 46.5 mm | Exterior dimensional anchor |
| Output-face pattern | M3 on 31 mm diameter circle | Four face holes anchored to photo/specification |
| Fixed mounting pattern | M4 on 40 mm diameter circle | Four backplate holes anchored to photo/specification |
| Locating pin | 3 mm diameter, 6 mm height | Modeled as a locating feature, not torque shaft |
| Magnetic poles | 20 pole pairs | Forty representative alternating magnet pole pieces |
| Speed constant | 100 rpm/V | Metadata, not measured simulation speed |
| Mass | 840 g | Source metadata; not a mass-property validation of this mesh |

The manufacturer explicitly assigns torque transmission to the shell's M3 face interface rather than the center pin. That instruction is why the new pulley is face-bolted. The S1/M8325S kit is documented by the manufacturer; a controller, power supply, brake resistor, encoder configuration, electrical harness and guards are not part of the present modeled drive stage.

The motor-characteristics source lists Kt = 0.083 Nm/A; 40 A free-air or 60 A forced-air continuous current, and 80 A for a three-second peak. It defines current as phase amplitude and voltage as line-to-line amplitude. It also warns that performance is approximate and cooling/application dependent. Do not substitute bus current, phase RMS current or the default D6374 calculator curve for those definitions.

## Inferred, not documented

The exterior shoulder/vent contours, wall thickness, stator lamination geometry, 36-slot arrangement, 14 displayed conductor turns per tooth, winding routing, insulation geometry, forty individual magnet shapes, magnet grade, air gap, backiron thickness, bearing dimensions/races/fits, shaft fits, electrical leads and connector envelope are modeled assumptions. The actual pole-pair count does not prove that the motor uses forty separately manufactured magnet blocks with the shapes modeled here.

Bearing balls are explicit geometry, but their cage/orbit/spin relationship is not simulated. The electromagnetic field, winding connection, cogging, phase currents, torque ripple, iron/copper losses, thermal behavior, preload and durability are not solved. The orange connector is a representative photo-based envelope, not an interchangeability-qualified LCC40 model. M3 holes are nominal bores and visual inserts, not fully modeled helical threads.

The geometry provenance is recorded per part and in the assembly manifest. The 181-count includes repeated conductor/ball/seam/marking nodes. It is a visual-component inventory, not a manufacturer's bill of materials.
