# Assembly and access

This is the intended physical sequence for the nominal study, not a fabrication instruction or load authorization. The checked removal trajectories and their exact scope are in `verification/review.json`.

1. Bolt the base crossmember, rear caster clamps, saddle, spine foot and side shoes. Insert the two sliding legs and the positive position pins. Fit front caster clamps. The rear stem nut is fitted through the open tube before closing the base; the sliding leg's underside slot clears the retained stem when retracted.
2. Attach the three rails to the spine. Mount guide blocks, brake compensation plates and brakes on their face carriages, then install the carriages. The unselected gas-assist assemblies and their retention must be resolved before any loaded height adjustment.
3. Attach each slotted root mounting block to its carriage with the four M6 screws, seat the shelf tongue in the slot, then fit the two central vertical M5 ties. Fit bushings into the original end plates. Bolt each link end tongue inside its closed tube, using the separate compression sleeves. Assemble the shoulder and elbow lap joints with their thrust washers, shoulder screws, spacers and nuts.
4. Assemble each head as a separate bench unit. Fit the moving pitch cheeks, short opposed shoulder screws, roll support and output plate. Fit tray pitch and roll clamp hardware. The tray webs and crossbeams support its plywood panel.
5. Attach unloaded heads to the arms. Equipment, harness and joint-lock adjustment follow only after the remaining component and load work in `ENGINEERING.md`.

## Reviewed access scope

The pivot sequences begin with equipment removed and the harness disconnected. Tray roll clamps are removed before tray roll-pivot service. The nut, shoulder screw, top spacer and full bushing are removed sequentially along the shaft axis. Full bushing extraction is checked, not just initial motion.

Pitch service is a detached-head bench operation. Wiring and tray clamp hardware are removed first. Remove the nut, short shoulder screw and outer spacer; remove the two bottom cheek screws; remove the independent fixed cheek; then withdraw the thrust washer and full flanged bushing. The review records any blocked trajectory. Prerequisite equipment, wiring and clamp removal is not certified by those pivot trajectories.

The exploded elbow viewer selects eight original parts. Tube bodies and their attachment screws are omitted from this inspection selection. Its artificial separated states are checked individually. The cutaway removes the Y-positive half of those exact solids and is bound to the complete source STEP. Neither derivative is a production assembly or an exploded removal procedure.
