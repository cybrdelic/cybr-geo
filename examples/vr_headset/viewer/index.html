CYBR GEO / MOBILE OPTICS LAB

VISOR M1

A stereo depth scene for the printed M1 headset. Rotate your phone to landscape, match the mechanical IPD, then enter VR.

Uses the specified 34 mm PMMA lens and two-interface Snell ray mapping. A purchased lens needs its own measured prescription. This prototype has orientation tracking; positional tracking is not implemented.
